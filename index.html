Featured case study

FlyRank AI — Content Performance Clustering

Segmented a 30,000-row content dataset into 6 actionable performance archetypes using a composite numeric + TF-IDF feature matrix and K-Means, validated against HDBSCAN.

Problem

FlyRank had 30K rows of content metrics and no clear strategy for what content to prioritize — the ask was just "find insights in the data."

What I did

Framed it as unsupervised clustering. Engineered a composite feature matrix (engagement ratios + TF-IDF tags), tested K-Means against HDBSCAN with silhouette validation, and deliberately excluded vanity metrics so clusters reflected true retention, not viral spikes.

What came of it

6 distinct performance archetypes, each documented in plain English so marketing leads could decide what to produce, pause, or iterate on.

Adult Income Prediction Web Engine

End-to-end ML system modeling demographic, education, and occupational attributes to predict whether annual income exceeds $50,000.

Preprocessing: LabelEncoder for categorical mapping, 80/20 train-test split with a fixed random state to prevent leakage, StandardScaler for distance-based models like KNN.

Engineered features: capital_net (capital.gain − capital.loss), hours_month (hours.per.week × 4), is_mature (binary split at age 35).
